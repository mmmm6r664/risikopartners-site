Who We Serve

Financial risk insight for your kind of balance sheet.

Financial risk looks different for a fund, a bank and a corporate treasury. Our advice reflects the exposures and pressures specific to you.

Request a Consultation

Asset Managers & Funds

Hedge funds, private credit and long-only managers need clear, independent measures of market, liquidity and counterparty risk.

  • Portfolio VaR & stress testing
  • Liquidity & redemption risk
  • Counterparty & prime broker exposure
  • Risk reporting for investors

Banks & Lenders

Community banks, credit unions and specialty lenders balance credit, interest rate and liquidity risk on every decision.

  • Credit portfolio concentration
  • Interest rate risk (IRRBB) & ALM
  • Liquidity stress testing
  • Exam & audit readiness

Family Offices & Wealth

Concentrated, multi-asset portfolios deserve institutional-grade risk insight without institutional bureaucracy.

  • Consolidated exposure reporting
  • Concentration & correlation risk
  • Scenario analysis
  • Investment risk policy

Corporate Treasury

CFOs and treasurers need to understand and hedge the financial risks hidden in revenue, costs and debt.

  • FX & commodity exposure
  • Debt & interest rate hedging
  • Cash & liquidity planning
  • Hedge policy & accounting support

Fintech & Payments

Fast-growing financial platforms face credit, liquidity and settlement risk that scales with every new customer.

  • Credit & loss modelling review
  • Settlement & funding risk
  • Safeguarding & reserves
  • Risk frameworks for growth

Insurance Investment Portfolios

Insurers' investment portfolios must balance yield, duration matching and credit quality.

  • Asset-liability duration matching
  • Credit quality & spread risk
  • Investment risk limits
  • Scenario & capital impact
