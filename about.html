Who We Are

Built on international banking and financial markets experience.

Risiko Partners is an independent global markets, treasury and financial risk advisory firm providing specialized strategic, operational and technical consulting services to corporations, banks and institutional clients.

Our expertise spans foreign exchange, interest rates, derivatives, fixed income, money markets, corporate treasury, asset and liability management, and financial risk management.

Drawing on extensive international banking and financial markets experience, we help organizations strengthen their treasury capabilities, enhance financial risk management frameworks, develop product capabilities and navigate increasingly complex global financial markets.
