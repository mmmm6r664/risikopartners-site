Industries

Sector insight, applied to your risk profile.

Risk looks different in every industry. Our advice reflects the exposures, regulations and pressures specific to your sector.

Financial Services

Asset managers, private funds, lenders and fintechs face fast-moving market, liquidity, credit and regulatory risk. We help them build frameworks that keep pace with growth.

  • Market & liquidity risk
  • Credit & counterparty exposure
  • Regulatory readiness
  • Model & data risk

Insurance

Carriers, MGAs and brokers balance underwriting, capital and operational risk. We bring an independent view to risk governance and controls.

  • Risk governance frameworks
  • Underwriting risk controls
  • Capital & solvency planning
  • Third-party oversight

Corporates

Enterprises with complex supply chains and global operations need visibility on risks that cross business lines.

  • Enterprise risk programs
  • Treasury & FX risk
  • Supply chain resilience
  • Board risk reporting

Real Estate & Infrastructure

Long-horizon assets carry concentrated financial, development and operational exposures.

  • Project & development risk
  • Financing & rate risk
  • Counterparty review
  • Portfolio stress testing

Energy & Commodities

Price volatility, physical operations and regulation combine into a demanding risk profile.

  • Commodity price risk
  • Hedging policy review
  • Operational resilience
  • Regulatory compliance

Private Equity & Growth Companies

Fast-growing businesses need risk discipline that scales without slowing them down.

  • Pre-investment risk diligence
  • Portfolio company risk programs
  • Governance build-out
  • Exit readiness
