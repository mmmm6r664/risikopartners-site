Services

Advisory across the full risk spectrum.

Six integrated capabilities, delivered by advisors who understand how risk connects across your organization.

Capabilities

What we deliver.

Engage us for a single assessment or an ongoing advisory relationship. Each service can stand alone or combine into a broader program.

01

Enterprise Risk Management

Build a risk framework that leadership actually uses — from risk appetite and taxonomy to registers, reporting and board-level oversight.

  • Risk appetite & tolerance statements
  • Risk identification & assessment workshops
  • Key risk indicators and dashboards
  • Board and committee reporting
02

Financial & Market Risk

Understand how market movements, liquidity and counterparties affect your balance sheet, and put sensible limits and controls around them.

  • Market and liquidity risk review
  • Counterparty & credit exposure analysis
  • Scenario analysis & stress testing
  • Treasury and hedging policy review
03

Regulatory & Compliance

Stay ahead of regulatory expectations with practical compliance programs, gap assessments and remediation support.

  • Regulatory gap assessments
  • Policy & procedure frameworks
  • Compliance monitoring programs
  • Exam and audit readiness
04

Operational Resilience

Prepare for disruption. We map critical processes, test your response and strengthen continuity across the organization.

  • Business impact analysis
  • Continuity & crisis planning
  • Third-party & vendor risk
  • Tabletop exercises
05

Governance & Controls

Align roles, policies and internal controls so accountability is clear and decisions are documented.

  • Three-lines-of-defense design
  • Internal control assessments
  • Policy governance
  • Risk culture review
06

Transaction & Strategic Risk

Independent risk perspective on major decisions — acquisitions, new markets, new products and capital commitments.

  • Pre-transaction risk diligence
  • New product & market risk review
  • Strategic risk assessment
  • Post-deal integration risk
