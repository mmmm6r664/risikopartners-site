Capabilities

What we deliver.

Engage us for a single assessment or an ongoing advisory relationship. Each service can stand alone or combine into a broader financial risk program.

01

Market Risk

Measure and control how price moves in equities, rates, credit spreads and commodities affect your portfolio and balance sheet.

  • Value-at-Risk & expected shortfall
  • Sensitivity (Greeks) analysis
  • Market risk limit frameworks
  • Daily risk reporting design
02

Credit & Counterparty Risk

Understand who owes you what, how concentrated those exposures are, and what happens if a counterparty fails.

  • Counterparty exposure measurement
  • Credit concentration analysis
  • Collateral & margin review
  • Credit limit & approval frameworks
03

Liquidity & Funding Risk

Make sure cash is there when you need it — in normal markets and under stress.

  • Cash-flow & liquidity forecasting
  • Liquidity buffer sizing
  • Contingency funding plans
  • Funding concentration review
04

Interest Rate & FX Risk

Quantify exposure to rate and currency movements and design hedging strategies that fit your objectives.

  • Asset-liability (ALM) analysis
  • Interest rate gap & duration
  • FX exposure mapping
  • Hedging strategy & policy review
05

Stress Testing & Scenarios

See how your portfolio and balance sheet hold up when markets break from the historical playbook.

  • Historical & hypothetical scenarios
  • Reverse stress testing
  • Capital & liquidity impact
  • Board-ready scenario reporting
06

Financial Risk Governance

Put the policies, limits, models and reporting in place so financial risk is owned, measured and escalated properly.

  • Risk appetite & limit setting
  • Financial risk policies
  • Model & valuation risk review
  • Risk committee reporting
